# 100 Toy-Machine Creative Tools — Idea Bank

Generated by a 10-lane idea council for the **Figma × Contra Config Makeathon** (June 2026 · Figma Weave AI + design agent beta · $100K prizes).

**The pattern every idea follows (the "STAMP formula"):** a fun, tactile, *non-app* interface where you physically MAKE something → you keep a real, shareable artifact → and the AI quietly solves the boring/hard part (seamless tiling, registration, the math, placement into a document) while you keep the handmade soul.

---

## ⭐ Curated top 12 (best STAMP siblings)

Chosen for: tactile-toy charm + a genuinely shareable artifact + an AI twist that kills real friction (not just "generates art").

1. **Terrazzo Toss** *(ceramics)* — fling marble chips onto a slab, grind to reveal cross-sections. Output = a **seamless tiling pattern** (wallpaper/laptop skin). Closest cousin to STAMP's "make a repeat" magic.
2. **Spiro-Gearbox** *(optical)* — snap real toothed gears onto a pegboard, drop a pen, crank out hypotrochoid line-art. AI picks gear ratios from a vibe word; exports plotter/sticker-ready SVG.
3. **Wax & Whisper Seals** *(stationery)* — pour molten wax, press-and-hold a brass die. The seal drops **onto any uploaded PDF/invite** and dents the paper. Directly extends STAMP's "stamp into a document" idea.
4. **Riso Drum** *(print)* — hand-crank a 2-color risograph; AI separates any image into believable spot-color layers with that off-by-2mm overprint glow.
5. **Stained-Glass Lightbox** *(optical)* — assemble glass shards into lead came, then drag the sun across the sky and watch colored light travel the wall (volumetric god-rays). Gorgeous demo.
6. **Punch Box** *(music)* — punch holes in a scrolling paper roll, crank to play a music box. AI fills a tasteful counter-melody so any pattern sounds intentional. Artifact = a re-punchable shareable card.
7. **Loop Pixie** *(fiber)* — thread a wooden loom and weave over/under by hand; AI completes the tedious middle rows in your rhythm into a seamless woven field. Output = tapestry + knit chart.
8. **Mosaic Smash** *(ceramics)* — tap to SMASH tiles into shards, drag-snap them along an image's contours, grout-flood to finish. AI auto-tessellates shards to follow the picture.
9. **Pack Ripper** *(capsule)* — pinch-tear a foil booster pack; out fan 5 holo trading cards about your life/pets/inside-jokes, with one guaranteed rainbow chase card.
10. **Notary Roller Stamp Desk** *(stationery)* — load a rubber stamp on the ink pad (runs dry over uses), THUNK it onto an **uploaded form**; AI designs the official round seal + date band and places it on the detected signature line.
11. **Light-Painter Wand** *(optical)* — swing a glow-wand through a dark frame (shutter stays open); AI steadies your scribble into elegant calligraphic light-strokes with bokeh + spark trails. Output = long-exposure photo + the build-reveal clip.
12. **Snow Globe Séance** *(diorama)* — drop a photo in a glass dome, shake your phone to set the weather; AI sculpts the flat photo into a layered 3D scene and choreographs the falling particles. Output = a "globe" message that snows when opened.

**Bonus insight:** the entire **Stationery lane** (wax seals, embosser, Dymo, postage, notary stamps, washi, calligraphy) all apply *into uploaded documents* — they're a natural **product family with STAMP**, not just rivals. STAMP could ship as "the carving tool" with these as companion stamps in the drawer.

## How to read this bank
- **Want the safest "STAMP-adjacent" win:** Terrazzo Toss, Loop Pixie, Mosaic Smash, or any Stationery-lane stamp (they all output a real artifact + solve genuine friction).
- **Want the biggest "wow" demo:** Stained-Glass Lightbox, Light-Painter Wand, Snow Globe Séance, Spiro-Gearbox.
- **Want strongest share-loop:** Pack Ripper, Gacha-Fortune, Snow Globe Séance, Knotwork.
- **Want a product family, not one toy:** ship STAMP as the carving studio + the Stationery lane (wax/embosser/Dymo/notary/postage) as companion stamps in the drawer.
